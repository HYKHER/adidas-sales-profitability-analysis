==> Adidas Sales Dashboard 

==> Project Overview

The Adidas Sales Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze Adidas sales performance, profitability, products, retailers, sales methods, and geographical performance.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of key business KPIs and helps identify high-performing products, cities, states, retailers, and sales channels.

==> Dashboard Preview

 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks the following key performance indicators:

- Total Sales: $120M
- Total Profit: $47M
- Average Price per Unit: $45
- Total Units Sold: 2M

 Key Analysis

==> 1. Sales by Product Category

The dashboard analyzes sales across three major product categories:

- Street Footwear – $45M
- Athletic Footwear – $40M
- Apparel – $35M

Street Footwear generated the highest sales among the product categories.

==> 2. Sales by State

The dashboard compares sales performance across different states.

Top-performing states include:

- New York – $8.7M
- California – $8.6M
- Florida – $7.8M
- Texas – $6.6M
- South Carolina – $3.6M
- Louisiana – $3.4M

New York generated the highest sales among the displayed states.

==> 3. Top 5 Cities by Profit

The dashboard identifies the top cities based on operating profit.

- New York – $2.1M
- Charleston – $2.0M
- San Francisco – $1.6M
- Miami – $1.6M
- Portland – $1.6M

==> 4. Monthly / Quarterly Sales Performance

Sales performance is analyzed across the four quarters:

- Q1 – $26M
- Q2 – $30M
- Q3 – $35M
- Q4 – $29M

Q3 recorded the highest sales at approximately $35M.

==> 5. Sales by Gender

The dashboard compares sales between Men and Women across product categories.

This helps understand customer demand and identify which product categories perform better for each gender segment.

==> 6. Sales by Sales Method

Sales are divided into three sales channels:

- Online – $45M
- Outlet – $40M
- In-store – $36M

Online sales generated the highest contribution among the displayed sales methods.

==> 7. Profit by Retailer

The dashboard compares operating profit generated by different retailers.

Top retailers include:

- West Gear – $12M
- Foot Locker – $11M
- Sports Direct – $11M
- Kohl's – $5M
- Amazon – $4M
- Walmart – $4M

West Gear generated the highest operating profit.

==> Business Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- Adidas generated approximately **$120M in total sales** and **$47M in operating profit**.
- Street Footwear is the highest-selling product category.
- New York is one of the strongest-performing states and cities.
- Q3 generated the highest quarterly sales.
- Online is the leading sales method.
- West Gear generated the highest operating profit among the displayed retailers.
- The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales and profitability across products, geography, customers, retailers, and sales channels.

==> Dashboard Features

- Interactive filters for Region, State, and City
- Time-based filtering by Year, Quarter, and Month
- KPI cards for overall performance
- Product category analysis
- State and city performance analysis
- Quarterly sales trend
- Gender-based sales comparison
- Sales method analysis
- Retailer profitability analysis

==> Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw Adidas sales data into an interactive dashboard that helps business users quickly understand sales performance, profitability, product trends, geographical performance, and sales-channel contribution.
